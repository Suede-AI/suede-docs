## Seven breakout repos. Seven ways to build something better.

Seven picks from our weekly breakout watch: projects created between November 8 and November 15, ordered by recorded GitHub stars in our November 15 snapshot.

### 1. agentes-chat · 1,414 stars
[agentes-chat/agentes-chat](https://github.com/agentes-chat/agentes-chat)

A self-hosted workspace where people and AI agents share channels and direct messages. Bring the work into one shared conversation instead of scattering it across individual agent sessions.

**Build with it:** set up a private agent workspace for a team. Package the onboarding and maintenance around it. Sell the working setup, not another tool they have to figure out.

### 2. lot-map · 840 stars
[alexeyw/lot-map](https://github.com/alexeyw/lot-map)

A live visual map of Claude Code's activity, including tool calls, hooks, skills and subagents.

**Put it to work:** make multi-agent workflows visible. Show clients what ran, trace what happened and debug the workflow without burying everyone in a wall of logs.

### 3. line-art-animation · 778 stars
[JDhruv14/line-art-animation](https://github.com/JDhruv14/line-art-animation)

A Claude skill for animated line-art scenes and characters.

**Build with it:** put an animated explanation of the product right on its landing page. Show the thing working. Give the visitor something better than another generic AI hero image.

### 4. ste80-humanizer · 621 stars
[Dodaios/ste80-humanizer](https://github.com/Dodaios/ste80-humanizer)

A writing skill aimed at removing familiar AI-writing patterns and producing clearer technical English.

**Put it to work:** clean up the AI-assisted drafts piling up across your agency. Cut the stock phrasing, sharpen the technical writing and keep the facts and the writer's voice intact.

### 5. cinematic-skill · 421 stars
[Moh4696/cinematic-skill](https://github.com/Moh4696/cinematic-skill)

Code-driven narrated films that coordinate camera movement, images, maps and cards with a voice track.

**Build with it:** product explainers, research summaries and launch videos with visuals that actually explain the idea. Make the camera, maps and narration work together.

### 6. guizang-game-skill · 343 stars
[op7418/guizang-game-skill](https://github.com/op7418/guizang-game-skill)

A skill for building playable 3D browser action games, with generated assets and a single-HTML-file delivery format.

**Build with it:** branded mini-games for launches, events and campaigns. Give people something to play with instead of another form to fill out.

### 7. article2media · 316 stars
[threerocks/article2media](https://github.com/threerocks/article2media)

Repurposes an article or transcript into formats including carousels, magazine-style graphics, WeChat layouts and voiced short videos.

**Build with it:** a recurring content service that turns one strong source piece into a full set of platform-ready assets. Adapt the idea to each format. Stop copying the same paragraph everywhere.

## 8 businesses to start today!

You have the tools. Now give them a job worth paying for. Here are eight offers to build, package and put in front of customers.

Choose your starting point with four questions: Who needs the result? Which tools cover the job? What still needs your judgment? Who will buy it? Start with the offer you can demonstrate and deliver best.

### 1. Launch a GEO and AI-visibility audit service
Find what stops AI search from understanding a business. Turn the findings into a prioritized fix list the owner can act on. Start with [seo-audit](https://suedeai.directory/coreyhaines31/marketingskills/seo-audit/) and [schema](https://suedeai.directory/coreyhaines31/marketingskills/schema/).

**The offer:** an audit, a prioritized action plan and clearer website information for search engines and AI agents. Give the owner the next steps, not a pile of jargon.

### 2. Build a lead-qualification service
Turn inbound enquiries into useful sales briefs: what the prospect needs, whether they're a fit and what the next action should be. Start with [revops](https://suedeai.directory/coreyhaines31/marketingskills/revops/).

**The offer:** turn a messy inbox into a sales-ready brief for each lead. Needs, fit, next action. Get the team out of sorting mode and into useful conversations.

### 3. Take repetitive finance admin off the team
Pick one job: transaction categorization, document collection or preparing exceptions for review. Build that workflow and make it repeatable.

**The offer:** organized records and an exception queue ready for review. Nail one recurring task first. That is a service you can explain, demonstrate and deliver.

### 4. Turn support chaos into a service
Organize incoming tickets by issue and urgency, prepare relevant context and draft the next response.

**The offer:** a prioritized queue with context and draft responses ready to review. Give the support team a head start on every ticket.

### 5. Package pre-launch security reviews
Build a pre-launch review service around [fp-check](https://suedeai.directory/trailofbits/skills/fp-check/) and [CodeQL](https://suedeai.directory/trailofbits/skills/codeql/), with an experienced reviewer evaluating the findings.

**The offer:** a clear findings report with specific investigation and fix priorities. Make it useful to the developer who has to act on it.

### 6. Start a short-form video production service
Build a repeatable production workflow with [Hyperframes](https://suedeai.directory/heygen-com/hyperframes/) for product explainers, announcements and educational clips.

**The offer:** a recurring video series built around the client's products and expertise. Consistent visuals. Specific ideas. A recognizable style across the whole series.

### 7. Sell better prospect research and outreach
Combine prospect research with [cold-email](https://suedeai.directory/coreyhaines31/marketingskills/cold-email/) to prepare specific, relevant outreach.

**The offer:** researched prospects and outreach that speaks to their actual business. Show why the fit makes sense. Give the recipient a reason to care.

### 8. Launch a landing-page sprint offer
Pair [Impeccable](https://suedeai.directory/pbakaus/impeccable/impeccable/) with [CRO](https://suedeai.directory/coreyhaines31/marketingskills/cro/) for focused landing-page work.

**The offer:** one focused landing page with a clear offer and a working conversion path. Define the audience and the action. Build the page around both. Ship it.

## Pick your stack. Give it a real job.

- **SEO + GEO:** seo-audit → schema. Find the website's problems, then structure its information more clearly.
- **Lead operations:** revops + cold-email. Organize prospect information and prepare outreach around a specific offer.
- **Landing pages:** Impeccable + CRO. Pair the design work with a clear audience, offer and conversion path.
- **Security review:** CodeQL → fp-check → Trailmark finding triage. Analyze the code, check findings and investigate reachability with a reviewer.
- **Video production:** Hyperframes + plot structure. Shape the story and build the visual sequence around it.

These are task stacks to assemble around your workflow. Pick one, run a sample and judge the output against the job.
